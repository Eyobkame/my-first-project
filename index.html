Why We Run at Sunrise

Every Saturday and Sunday morning, local runners meet before sunrise to enjoy cool mountain air, eucalyptus-lined dirt paths, and steady elevation training.

What to Bring on Your First Run

  • Trail or cushioned road running shoes with good grip
  • A lightweight windbreaker for cool morning temperatures
  • At least 500ml of water and a small snack
  • A fully charged phone or running watch
